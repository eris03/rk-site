Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how RK Developers collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, fill in our inquiry form, or interact with our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Full name, phone number, and email address
  • Property interests and budget preferences
  • Messages and requirements submitted through our contact form
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited (via cookies and analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your property inquiries and schedule site visits
  • Send you relevant updates about our projects and offers
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All form submissions are transmitted securely and stored with restricted access.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and to analyze site traffic. You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features of our website may not function correctly without them.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services such as Google Analytics to process data on our behalf. These services are bound by strict data protection agreements and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you

  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
