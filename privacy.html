Privacy Policy

RK Developers · Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information RK Developers collects from people who contact us or submit an enquiry through our website or our advertisements, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly when you submit an enquiry form, request a site visit, or contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp. This typically includes:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Any details you choose to share about the property you are looking for, such as budget, preferred location or timeline

When you reach us through an online advertisement, the advertising platform (for example Google) may also tell us which campaign or ad your enquiry came from. We do not receive your browsing history from these platforms.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and share project details, pricing and availability
  • To arrange and follow up on site visits
  • To contact you about RK Developers projects that match what you asked about
  • To meet our legal, accounting and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share your details only where it is needed to serve your enquiry — with our own sales team, and with service providers who help us operate, such as our website host, customer-management software and advertising platforms. These providers may process your information only on our instructions. We may also disclose information where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to serve your request and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records. If you ask us to delete your details, we will do so unless we are required to keep them by law.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Write to us at the email address below and we will act on your request.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version on this page with a new date at the top.
